## What burnout is

Burnout is what happens when stress runs high for a long time without enough recovery. It often shows up as three things: exhaustion, a growing distance from your work, and a sense that nothing you do is enough.

For many Malayalis, work pressure comes bundled with other weights: supporting family back home, visas and job security abroad, long commutes, or the expectation to keep climbing. It can feel like there's no room to slow down.

## How therapy can help

Therapy won't make your workload disappear, but it can help you:

- understand what's draining you most, and what's within your control
- set boundaries that protect your time and energy, and practise keeping them
- quieten the inner critic that says you should always be doing more
- think clearly about bigger decisions, like a role change, a move or a break

## Something you can try today

At the end of your workday, write down three things: what you finished, what can wait until tomorrow, and one thing you're looking forward to this evening. Then close the laptop. A small ritual can help your mind clock out too.
