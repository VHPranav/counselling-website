## What anxiety is

Anxiety is your body's alarm system. It's designed to protect you, getting you ready to act when something feels risky. The trouble is that the alarm can become oversensitive: going off when there's no real danger, or staying on long after a stressful moment has passed.

For many Malayalis, anxiety gets tangled up with expectations: exams, careers, marriage, family reputation, life abroad. It can be hard to talk about at home, especially if "tension" is seen as something you should simply handle on your own.

## How therapy can help

A therapist won't tell you to "just relax". Instead, you'll work together to:

- understand what sets your anxiety off, and what keeps it going
- learn practical ways to calm your body in the moment, like slow breathing
- notice anxious thought patterns and test them, gently
- slowly face things you've been avoiding, at a pace that feels manageable

Approaches such as cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) are widely used for anxiety. Your therapist will explain how they work and adapt it to you.

## Something you can try today

Breathe in through your nose for four seconds, then out through your mouth for six. Making the out-breath longer than the in-breath signals to your body that it's safe to slow down. Try it for two minutes, or use the [breathing circle on our home page](/en).
