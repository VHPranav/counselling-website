## What depression is

Depression is more than a bad mood, and it isn't a weakness or a lack of faith. It affects how you think, feel and function, and it can make simple things, like replying to a message or getting out of bed, feel enormous.

It looks different from person to person. Some people feel deep sadness. Others feel mostly tired, irritable or flat. Many carry on working and caring for family while feeling hollow inside.

## How therapy can help

Talking with a therapist gives you a steady, non-judgemental space to:

- put words to what you're feeling, without being rushed or told to "think positive"
- notice the thoughts that pull your mood down, and loosen their grip
- rebuild small routines and moments of meaning, one step at a time
- look at what's happening in your life, such as relationships, losses or pressures, that may be feeding the low mood

If your therapist feels a medical opinion could help, they'll talk it through with you. We don't prescribe medication, but we can help you understand your options.

## Something you can try today

Pick one very small thing, like a short walk, a shower, or a message to a friend, and do just that. Depression shrinks motivation. Small actions often come before feeling better, not after.
