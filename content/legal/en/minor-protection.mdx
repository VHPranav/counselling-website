We offer counselling to teenagers aged 13 to 17. Young people deserve a safe, respectful space to talk, and parents and guardians deserve to know how that space works. This policy explains both.

## Who it covers

This policy applies to anyone under 18 who uses our services. We don't offer sessions to children under 13 through this website; please contact us directly to discuss options.

## Consent from a parent or guardian

Before a teen's first session, a parent or lawful guardian must give consent. We collect the guardian's name, relationship to the teen and contact details, and may take reasonable steps to verify them, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We also want the teen's own agreement. Therapy works best when a young person has chosen it.

## Privacy for teens

Teens need to feel safe to speak openly. In the first session, the therapist will agree with the teen and the guardian what stays private and what may be shared. Usually, guardians are told about attendance and general progress, not the details of what's discussed.

## When we will share information

We will break confidentiality to keep a young person safe. This includes:

- any serious risk of harm to the teen or someone else
- any suspicion that a child is being abused, which we report as required by law, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012

Where it's safe to do so, the therapist will tell the teen before sharing anything.
