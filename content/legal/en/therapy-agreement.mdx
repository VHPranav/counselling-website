This agreement explains how therapy with Thanal works, so you know what to expect before you begin. Your therapist will go through it with you in your first session, and you can ask questions at any time.

## What therapy is, and isn't

Therapy is a collaborative process. Your therapist will listen, help you understand what you're experiencing, and work with you on ways forward. Results differ from person to person, and no particular outcome can be guaranteed. Therapy can sometimes bring up difficult feelings before things feel easier.

Our therapists don't prescribe medication or provide emergency care. If they think a medical opinion would help, they'll discuss it with you.

## Confidentiality

What you share in sessions is confidential, in line with professional ethics and Indian law, including the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017. Your therapist will not share it without your consent, except where:

- there is a serious and immediate risk of harm to you or someone else
- a child or vulnerable person may be at risk of abuse, including where reporting is required under the POCSO Act, 2012
- a court or the law requires disclosure
